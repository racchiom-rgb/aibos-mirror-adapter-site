AIBOS Mirror Adapter

Google Drive integration for a privately operated AIBOS knowledge workflow.

HomePrivacy Policy

What the app does

AIBOS Mirror Adapter supports copying designated local knowledge documents to a Google Drive mirror and checking the resulting files against the local source. Related operations using the same Google authorization configuration can make limited additions to Google Docs.

Who uses it

This integration is intended for the owner's own use. It is not distributed as a public application or offered as a service to other users.

Google account access

The integration requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive, a restricted Google Drive scope that can grant access across the authorized Google Drive. Its intended operations are limited to the designated documents and related workflows; the OAuth scope itself is broader than those operations.

Access is subject to Google account authorization. Details of how information is handled are described in the Privacy Policy.
